It’s not social. It’s television.

We still say "social network."

Facebook started as a directory. Twitter was a conversation. Instagram was a photo album.

Then attention paid better than connection. Almost nobody posts. Everyone else scrolls.

It's not social. It's television.

We've never been more connected. Or more alone. The apps keep the thumb moving. They don't help two people meet on a street corner.

That's where WeWalk starts. So you don't walk home alone.

Mehdi Baï

Lille
